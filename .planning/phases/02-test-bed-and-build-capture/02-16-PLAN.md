<objective>
Fix review warnings WR-02 and WR-08 (founder request in this gap closure; no UAT gap id). WR-02: tie replay to the manifest pin and validate the commit path component. WR-08: enforce MIN_RETAINED in the step that produces the evidence and require the evidence fields for `cause = differs`.

Purpose: replay cannot report `identical` for a capture made from another pin or with other tools, and the full-bed run cannot pass with fewer than 10 targets or with a `differs` drop that records nothing.
Output: pin check and pinned pristine path in testbed.rs, `pinned_source` in fetch.rs, a shared reported-tools helper in capture.rs, the floor in load_inputs, the `differs` rule in droplog.rs, tests.
</objective>
