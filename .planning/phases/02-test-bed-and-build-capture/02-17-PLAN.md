3. Poll every 60 s (one Bash call is limited to 10 minutes) until `testbed` and `ci` are `completed`. A run still going 15 minutes after its `timeout-minutes` is stuck: checkpoint:human-action. `container` is triggered by crates/** and is not awaited; record its conclusion if it has finished.
4. Jobs: `gh api repos/sat-wik/migrationtool/actions/runs/<run_id>/jobs --jq '.jobs[] | [.id, .name, .conclusion] | @tsv'`.
