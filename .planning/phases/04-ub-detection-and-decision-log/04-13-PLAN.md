## Facts

- crates/mt-harness/src/exec.rs: `Request::entry(input, out_cap, scalars)` (70) builds the target request; `run_driver` (168) runs it; 04-07's `variant::replay_vector` runs a request against a host variant; 04-09's `relabel::choose_label` chooses the label; mt-vectors `write_corpus` writes a corpus directory with its index.
- The `.ktest` object `in` holds the buffer, `len` the length (u32 little-endian in the object bytes on x86-64), and one object per symbolic scalar named after the scalar (from 04-12's harness).
- KLEE floor candidates (planner choice: entries with buffer, length and scalar roles and few states): corejson, microcoap, cjson, tinycbor; the executor adds manifest-order targets until at least 3 have `completed` or `timeout` results.
